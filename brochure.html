The Designer

M started sewing clothes as a child since what she desired to wear wasn't in stores, learning the technical skills to create her vision.

Growing up in rural Louisiana with a self-directed education shaped both her technical independence and creative perspective.

In her 20s, everything changed when she made a leather garment as part of a Mardi Gras costume in New Orleans. She loved it, surprised by joy as it became more beautiful with wear instead of breaking down—timeless, practical, and expressive.

Slow Fashion, designed and constructed from start to finish by the artist with a focus on fit, movement, and longevity.

“From wild animals, these hides are so close to nature you can sense the earthy aliveness, feel it like the comfort of a hug, decadently supple.”
